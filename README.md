# glist

A fast, thumb-friendly grocery list that runs entirely in your browser. No backend, no accounts, no build step.

**[Open glist](https://sri.github.io/glist/)**

## Highlights

- **One-handed shopping** — big tap targets designed for pushing a cart
- **Store groups** — items organized by store, collapse/expand as you shop, move a store to the top when you walk in
- **Restock predictions** — learns your purchase cadence and flags items as "due soon" or "overdue" so you never forget the things you buy regularly
- **Smart catalog** — filter by Needs Purchase, In Cart, Recent, Never Bought, Old, or Just Removed
- **Multi-store items** — assign items to one or more stores with brand-colored chips
- **Purchase history** — tracks when you last bought each item with expandable detail view
- **Dark mode** — automatic, based on your system preference
- **100% local** — everything lives in `localStorage`, nothing leaves your device
