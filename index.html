The way I shop

glist models how I (sri) do grocery shopping.

Sometimes I need to look at the entire list of items to be reminded of what to buy. And other times, a thought comes and I have to quickly add it.

There are some items I can buy at any store. And others, that I can to buy at few different stores. So, when they're in the shopping cart, if I mark them as purchased at one store, I want them to be marked across all stores. So in the UI, when there are stores, I can just want to focus on what to buy at that particular store (hence the store grouping and ability to just focus on that single store).

Also, when I'm shopping, I'm pushing around a big, old cart. And so, I want huge buttons that I can click without much effort and focus (looking at you Google Keep). And sometimes, I just want to bubble up what's remaining to purchase so that it is easier to see -- hence, the "Move purchased to bottom" button.

I also love colors and themes. So I have a bunch of those.

All the code here was done using Codex and Claude.
